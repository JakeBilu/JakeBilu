## Health Space Design Philosophy

Every residential and commercial project across **Johor Bahru, Iskandar Puteri, Mount Austin, Eco Botanic, Horizon Hills, Skudai, and Bukit Chagar (RTS Link)** is built on measurable architectural and ergonomic standards:

- **Clear Anthropometric Circulation:** `>= 900 mm` clear walkways and `>= 1,200 mm` staircase landings
- **Tropical Material Durability:** Low-VOC finishes, `18 mm` E0/ENF moisture-resistant or aluminium wet-kitchen carcasses
- **Verified Site QA:** Mandatory 24–48 hour bathroom waterproofing ponding tests prior to tiling and structured photo/video progress logs for cross-border Singapore homeowners
